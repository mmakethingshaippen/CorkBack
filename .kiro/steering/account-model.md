# Account Model — CorkBack

## Public browsing, no account wall

- Anyone can browse the map and open postings with no account.
- An account is required only to post, reply, or set up alerts.

## Accounts

- **Email login only.** Social logins are deferred until after the contest.
- Judges sign up with email like a regular user. There is no demo-login frame, no Board-view frame, no Board/Map toggle.

## Identity and privacy

- **Username is the only public name.** No full name is collected or shown.
- **Email stays private.** Never shown publicly.
- **User exact location is never shown publicly** (see location-rules.md).

## Username rules

- Unique across the system.
- 3 to 20 characters.
- Letters, numbers, and underscore only. No spaces.
- (These rules are covered by at least one property-based test in a later stage.)
